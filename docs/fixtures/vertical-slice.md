# Vertical-slice fixture v1 (fictional, provisional)

`VERTICAL_SLICE_FIXTURE_VERSION = 1` in `packages/scenarios/src/vertical-slice.ts` identifies the illustrative dataset and behavior. This is not product approval or M1 acceptance. Changing default commodities, cohort mix, route, budgets, policies, or trade rules requires a fixture-version bump and repeatability review. The core engine's separate schema/rules versions retain their own compatibility rules.

- Exactly two kingdoms: `north` (player), `south` (autonomous AI). Two cities: `north-port`, `south-market`.
- Five illustrative commodities, in order: `grain`, `wood`, `tools`, `stone`, `cloth`. Grain and tools participate in local production/consumption, grain crosses the road, wood is a production input; stone and cloth are registered inventory/commodity examples, not a claim of five active production chains. No historical era is inferred.
- Two cohort labels: `farmers` and `craftspeople`, one in each city, four workers available in each. They are simplified cohort groups, not individuals or an approved population distribution. Their recurring needs are grain and tools respectively. Migration, births, deaths, skills and services belong to later work.
- A road from `north-port` to `south-market` costs three coin and takes two ticks. It carries one grain shipment, funded through the player's treasury transfer (10 coin to the north benefit account on tick 2; the transfer precedes the tick-3 freight debit). AI trade acceptance is computed independently from observable reserves; the player choice is an explicit scenario command. Refusal by either prevents the shipment. The road is not a comprehensive bilateral trade agreement.
- Default seed 42, 1000 ticks. The first 20 ticks have finite recipe inputs; later ticks still run all registered phases and can accumulate unmet needs, but do not imply uninterrupted output. The state/event hashes compare identical versioned configuration, seed, commands, runtime and rules, not economic correctness.

`runVerticalSlice({ seed, ticks, commodities, cohorts, road, playerChoice, aiMinimumStock })` allows alternate fictional values. Exactly five distinct commodity IDs and two distinct cohort IDs are required; road cost is 1–10 coin (matching the fixed 10-coin grant), duration positive. `aiMinimumStock > 0` makes the South AI refuse the grain offer because it owns no available grain. The CLI supports `run --scenario vertical-slice --seed 42 --ticks 1000`; programmatic options are intentionally not CLI flags in this slice.
