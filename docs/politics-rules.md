# Social action rules (T15)

Collective action is decided independently per configured group from explicit organization, resources, real-wage, unemployment and satisfaction signals. A group must have organization >= 50, resources >= 10, a positive demand and at least two hardship indicators (real wage < 8500 bps, unemployment >= 1500 bps, satisfaction <= 35). Decisions retain the observed signals, alternatives, constraints and reason. No political institutions, elections, parties or SC-11 claim are implied.

A proposal is observable for one tick before activation. Active strike actions reduce participating producer batch capacity by the configured participation share; protest is a distinct action with no automatic production stoppage. `registerPolitics` publishes a snapshot-safe impact plan; the economy alone consumes that plan. Negotiation commands validate their entire offer (including a wage increase no greater than group demand) before mutation. An affordable-offer refusal or inability to fund a valid accepted offer is logged as `failed`; malformed commands reject atomically. Funded settlement payments require explicitly identified payer/recipient account and owner bindings and use accounting transactions. Accepted wage concessions are bounded future batch-wage increments, not free money. Resolved actions recover capacity linearly over configured recovery ticks; active and negotiating actions expire at their configured duration. Terminal actions do not immediately re-propose under unchanged signals. Tax effect is explicitly `none-until-settlement`: any tax change is only whatever existing tax policies actually settle on wages/sales; this package does not forecast a tax delta or create a tax transaction.

When live economy and city state are present, unemployment, current income, and unmet-needs data inform hardship; real-wage level remains anchored to the configured group benchmark because the current live state has no real-wage index. Without linked live state, configured signals are a documented static fixture fallback. Group producer/cohort bindings and concession account ownership are checked when live economy exists. This bounded implementation has no direct tax-policy integration, political institutions, elections or SC-11 coverage.
